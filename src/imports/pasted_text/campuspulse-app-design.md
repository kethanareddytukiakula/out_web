Design a complete, production-ready mobile application called **CampusPulse**.

CampusPulse is a smart student lifestyle platform designed specifically for college students. It combines outing management, spending analytics, social discovery, hotspot tracking, student communities, and safety features into one unified experience.

The goal is to help students make smarter decisions about where they go, how they spend, whom they meet, and how they manage their campus life.

Create a premium, startup-level UI/UX similar to modern apps like Notion, Instagram, Spotify, and Google Maps, while maintaining a clean Material 3 design language.

Design Principles:

* Modern Material 3
* Premium startup aesthetic
* Mobile-first
* Clean white backgrounds
* Blue gradient accents
* Rounded cards
* Smooth micro-interactions
* Professional typography
* Data-rich dashboards
* High usability
* Student-focused design
